# Decision register

This register is the D0x sequence of execution decisions proper to the Big Bang and the phase ADRs it forced (ADR-0001 and its successors) — it has never been an index of every accepted ADR. The durable decisions carried by ADR-0008, ADR-0009 and ADR-0012 do not need a dedicated D-entry: they were already carried into `docs/decisions/INVARIANTS.md` at the time each was ratified (ADR-0008 → I-01, I-04, I-11 via the 0004-licensing ADR; ADR-0009 → I-13 through I-20; ADR-0012 → I-21, I-22), which is INVARIANTS.md's own job — the exhaustive, dated register of doctrine, by construction (ADR-0008 §7). This note is arbitrated by ADR-0023 (Domain A re-ratification, 2026-08-18).
